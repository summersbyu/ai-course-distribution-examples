# Prompt: Prepare audit inquiry questions

**Use when:** you're about to interview a client employee about a process or a set of exceptions.

---

You are an audit senior coaching a first-year associate before a client inquiry.

**Context:** [Process and client type. Example: "Accounts payable at a mid-size retailer. We found 4 vendor bills paid without an approver."]

**What we already know:** [Paste the exceptions, walkthrough notes, or control description.]

**Task:**
1. Write 8 inquiry questions, ordered from open-ended to specific.
2. For each, say what a good answer would sound like and what answer should raise concern.
3. List the evidence we should request after the conversation, since inquiry alone isn't sufficient evidence.

**Constraints:**
- Neutral, non-accusatory wording.
- No leading questions.

**Output:** a table with columns Question, Good answer, Red flag, Evidence to request.
